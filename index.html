Common questions

Do I need to create an account?

No. Open the site and start. Your progress is saved automatically in your browser.

Is it really free?

Yes — every quiz, test, lesson, and study tool. No ads, no upsells, no credit card.

What grade levels does it cover?

Math: arithmetic (grades 4–6) through college-level real analysis. English: grades 6–12. Most students between grades 6 and 12 are the sweet spot.

How is the AI tutor different from ChatGPT?

Max is built into every quiz, lesson, and study tool — he sees the exact question you're working on, knows what subject and grade you're studying, and can explain a missed answer or walk you through a section without you copying anything around. He won't give answers during a graded quiz, only after.

Can I use Atrium on my phone?

Yes. The site works in any modern mobile browser. No app to install.

Will my progress sync across devices?

Right now progress is per-browser (saved locally on your device). Cross-device sync via sign-in is coming soon.

What if I get stuck on a problem?

Every quiz has a "💬 Ask Max" button. After a missed answer he'll walk you through the correct approach with a worked example and a diagram if it helps.

How do I report a bug or ask for a feature?

Email hello@atriuminstitute.ai — we read everything.

Last updated: May 17, 2026

The short version: Atrium Institute is a tutoring website used by middle- and high-school students, including children under 13. We collect the minimum information needed to run an account-based learning service. We do not sell or rent your information. We do not show advertisements. We use a small number of trusted third parties (Anthropic for the AI tutor, Resend for email, and Render for hosting / database) to operate the service. Parents of children under 13 must consent to their child's account by linking through the in-product flow before the child can use the service.

1. Who we are

"Atrium Institute" ("we", "us", "our") operates the website at atriuminstitute.ai ("the Service"). This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information about people who use the Service ("you"). If you are under 18, please read this with a parent or guardian. If you are under 13, your parent or legal guardian must consent through the in-product parent linking flow before you can use the Service.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you give us

  • Email address. Required to create an account. Used to send sign-in codes and (if you opt in) study reminders.
  • Role. Whether you sign up as a student or a parent.
  • Age and state of residence. Asked at sign-up so we can apply the correct children's-privacy rules and determine whether parental consent is required.
  • Profile details (optional). If you fill out your profile: display name, school name, grade level, subjects of focus, courses you plan to study, a free-text study goal, your timezone, and your email-reminder preferences.
  • For parents: display name, relationship to the student (parent, guardian, tutor, or other), and weekly digest preferences.
  • Questions you ask Max. The AI tutor receives the text of your question and the surrounding context (course, topic, the question you're working on if any) so it can respond. We do not save a permanent transcript of individual chats beyond short-term operational logs used to investigate errors.

2.2 Information generated by your use of the Service

  • Quiz attempts. Each completed quiz is recorded with the course, topic, section, score, pass/fail, attempt number, duration, and the per-question answers you submitted (along with the correct answer). This is what powers your activity history and your linked parent's dashboard.
  • Activity events. A timeline of meaningful actions (sign-in, quiz pass, quiz fail, started a Max lesson, started a Study with Max session, parent / student linked or unlinked, reminder email sent).
  • Session cookie. A single HTTP-only cookie keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. It contains a random session token, not your personal data.
  • Server logs. Standard operational logs: timestamp, request path, response status, and approximate IP (held briefly for rate-limit enforcement and security). We do not retain detailed access logs beyond what's needed to operate the Service.

2.3 Information we do not collect

We do not run advertising trackers or third-party analytics pixels. We do not use fingerprinting. We do not collect precise location. We do not collect device identifiers, contact lists, photos, microphone, or camera data.

3. Children's privacy (COPPA and state laws)

Atrium Institute is intended as a study tool for students, and we know children under 13 will use it. In the United States, the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) requires us to obtain verifiable parental consent before collecting personal information from a child under 13. Several US states (including California, Colorado, and Connecticut) impose additional or stricter requirements for users under various age thresholds. We follow COPPA's federal baseline today, and we layer state-specific protections as our compliance posture matures.

What this means in practice:

  • When a user signs up as a student, we ask for their age. If the reported age is under 13, the account is created but placed in a consent-required state. The student cannot access courses, quizzes, or Max chat until a parent or legal guardian has linked to the account through our in-product linking flow.
  • The parent's act of signing up, providing their own email, and entering the child's link code constitutes the parent's affirmative consent to (a) the creation and continued operation of the child's account, (b) the storage of the categories of information described in section 2, and (c) the use of Anthropic's API to power the AI tutor for the child's account.
  • Email reminders to under-13 students are off by default and can only be enabled by a linked parent from their dashboard. The child cannot enable reminders for themselves.
  • Parents may at any time review the categories of information collected from their child by viewing the child's profile and activity through their dashboard, request deletion of the child's account by emailing hello@atriuminstitute.ai, or withdraw consent and have all personal information associated with the child's account deleted within 30 days.
  • We do not knowingly disclose a child's personal information to any third party except (a) the AI service provider strictly to generate the tutor's response, (b) the email service provider strictly to deliver a verification code or a parent-authorised reminder, and (c) the hosting and database providers that operate the Service. None of these providers is permitted to use the information for their own purposes.
  • We do not condition a child's participation in any activity on the disclosure of more personal information than is reasonably necessary to participate in that activity.

If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13 without proper consent, please contact us immediately at hello@atriuminstitute.ai and we will delete the information promptly.

4. How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Operate the Service: authenticate you, save your progress, render your activity timeline, populate the parent dashboard, and let parents see only their own children's progress.
  • Power the AI tutor: relay your question and the relevant academic context to the AI provider so it can produce a tutoring response.
  • Send transactional emails: sign-in verification codes, and (if you've opted in and, for under-13 students, only after a parent has authorised it) study reminder emails or parent weekly digests.
  • Enforce limits and prevent abuse: per-IP and per-day rate limits on AI calls, single-use email verification codes, and similar measures.
  • Improve the Service: anonymised, aggregated patterns (for example, which sections are most often failed) may inform content improvements. We do not use the substance of any individual student's quiz answers for that purpose.

5. Third-party service providers

We rely on a small set of third parties to run the Service. Each is bound by contract or by their published terms to use information only to provide their service to us:

  • Anthropic, PBC — provides the Claude language model that powers the AI tutor. Question text and surrounding academic context are sent to Anthropic's API. Per Anthropic's published policies, API inputs and outputs are not used to train their models. See Anthropic's privacy notice.
  • Resend — sends our transactional emails (sign-in codes, study reminders, parent digests). Resend receives the recipient's email address and the email's content.
  • Render — hosts the web service and the Postgres database where all account information, profiles, activity events, and quiz attempts are stored. Render does not access this data for any purpose other than to operate the platform we run on.

We do not transfer your information to any other third party for any reason, except where required by law (see section 8).

6. How long we keep information

  • Account, profile, quiz attempts, and activity log: kept for as long as the account is active. If you (or, for a child, your parent) request deletion, we erase it within 30 days.
  • Verification codes: expire after 15 minutes and are then automatically deleted.
  • Sessions: the session cookie has a 30-day maximum lifetime; the corresponding row in our sessions table is deleted on logout or expiry.
  • Email send records (at our provider Resend): retained for a short window per Resend's policies, used for deliverability troubleshooting.
  • Server logs: rotated regularly; not retained long-term.

7. Security

We take a security posture appropriate to a small educational service. Specifically:

  • All traffic is served over HTTPS.
  • Sign-in uses one-time, single-use, 15-minute codes; we do not store user-chosen passwords.
  • Sessions use HTTP-only, SameSite=Lax cookies; the session token is never exposed to JavaScript or to other domains.
  • Our Anthropic and Resend API keys live only on the server and are stored encrypted in the hosting platform's environment variables.
  • The database is hosted on a managed Postgres service with encryption at rest.
  • We apply rate limits and a daily spending cap on the AI API so a compromised account cannot drive runaway costs.

No service is perfectly secure. If we ever suffer a breach that affects your account, we will notify you in accordance with applicable law.

Subject to applicable law, you (or, for a child, the child's parent or guardian) may:

  • Export a copy of your account data in a portable format. Email us and we'll send a JSON export within 30 days.
  • Withdraw consent at any time. For a child's account, the parent's withdrawal results in deletion of the child's account.
  • Opt out of email reminders at any time. Every reminder and digest email carries an unsubscribe link. You can also turn reminders off from your profile page at any time.

About Atrium Institute

A calm place to learn math & English.

Atrium Institute is a self-paced learning platform built for middle and high school students who want to actually understand the material — not just rack up streaks and badges. We cover math from Pre-Algebra through Calculus and English Language Arts from grades 6 through 12, all inside one calm, distraction-free interface designed to support deep, focused work instead of constant interruptions.

Each course is organized into topics, and each topic into smaller quiz sections — typically three per topic — followed by a cumulative test covering the whole topic, and at the end of every course, a comprehensive final exam drawing from every section you've studied. Quizzes auto-grade themselves with detailed feedback on each answer, and your progress saves automatically as you go. There are over 100 topics and nearly 10,000 questions across the platform, ranging from quick conceptual checks to multi-step word problems and full literary analysis prompts.

At the center of Atrium Institute is Max, an AI tutor available 24/7 inside the app. Max isn't a chatbot tacked onto a quiz site — he's woven throughout the learning experience. Get a wrong answer and Max walks you through the mistake, explaining the underlying concept in plain language. Stuck on a topic? Click "Study [section name]" and Max will quiz you through it interactively, using whichever study technique fits the material best. Want a lesson before starting a quiz? Hit "Learn" and Max writes a tailored explanation in seconds.

We built Atrium Institute around the idea that real learning is about habits, not just exposure. The Study Methods panel includes ten research-backed techniques — Active Recall, the Feynman Technique, Spaced Repetition, the Pomodoro Method, Cornell Notes, Interleaved Practice, and more — each with interactive tools built in. There's a customizable Pomodoro timer with persistent countdown, a spaced-repetition planner that calculates exactly when you should review, a guided active-recall timer, and one-click access to ask Max to drill you on any topic using your chosen method.

Atrium Institute is for students who want to feel prepared, focused, and in control of their own learning — and for parents who want a real tutor available on a Sunday night without scheduling, at a fraction of the cost of a private one-on-one. We don't gamify, we don't run ads, and we don't sell your data. The interface is intentionally quiet so the work itself can take center stage. Whether you're catching up, working ahead, or studying for a specific exam, Atrium Institute is built to meet you where you are and help you get where you want to go.
